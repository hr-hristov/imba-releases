Update from Settings, Upgrade
Concept maps

Grow an idea as a mind map

Run /concept and the agent grows your idea as a map you mark up branch by branch. Every tool for the selected branch now sits on one bar of keys.

Triggers

Spawns set up their own triggers

Ask a spawn to keep an eye on something, and it makes the trigger itself. The trigger shows on the spawn's row and in its Triggers panel.

New spawn

Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, and the branch under Repo

New spawn offers the new models, and picks the branch to work on right under Repo itself.

Graphics cards

Browsers, instances and emulators on the graphics card

Turn the graphics card on in Settings and see what runs on it. The machine monitor and the bottom bar show how busy the card is.

Pinned repositories

A pinned repository stays in Ultrafocus

Ultrafocus New lists only repositories with a recent spawn. Pin one from its menu in Settings, Repositories, or with k in Ultrafocus, and New keeps listing it.
