State

Where a spawn stands, always current

The spawn's own agent writes its State card at the end of every turn. State on the newest turn, or c, opens it at once with every picture the spawn handed over, and sends the agent nothing.

Concepts

Move around a large concept map

Find, or /, goes to each branch whose name or document holds a word. Pan, or p, moves the map under a cross with the arrows, and on the whole map Ctrl+F numbers the main branches.

Concepts

Read a concept branch as a document

Read, or Enter, opens a branch's words on a screen of their own, laid out as a document with headings and lists. A tap on any line opens a note on it, and q goes back to the map.
