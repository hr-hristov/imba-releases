Update from Settings, Upgrade
Groups

A group of spawns

Group in New spawn makes a leader with members that work for it, three to start and up to eight, each with its own agent, model and effort. You talk only to the leader, which hands out the work. Its page carries one cell per spawn, and a member waiting on an approval is answered from its own page.

Documents

Document check

/rd reads a set of business documents, such as a tender's proposal, specification and price offer, and finds where they contradict each other. The card lists each question with the statements that clash and the document that decides it, fixes only mechanical slips as tracked changes, and says what it could not read. It is set up on Settings, Document check.

Document card

A bot's edits, as a page

When a spawn edits a Word or markdown document, its turn carries a document card. Unfold, or v, draws the document as a page with inserted words green and deleted words struck, who made each change and when, and n and p step through the changes.

Upgrade

Upgrading Claude Code and Codex

Settings, Upgrade now lists the machine's Claude Code and Codex beside their newest stable release. Upgrade starts a spawn that installs the new one and checks it answers, and the row links that spawn.

Logins

Locking a login

Each Claude login on the Agent usage panel has a lock at the end of its row. A locked login is drawn grey, and no automatic spawn lands on it, so you can keep one for your own work. On a laptop, l numbers the rows and a digit locks one.

Mods

Mods

Settings, Mods lists the code imba puts inside Claude Code: Closing asks, Spawn status, and Prompts and stops. Each row says whether the installed Claude Code runs it, the text it adds to each step and how many Claude spawns run it. Its switch leaves it out of new spawns at once.

Also

  • A message to a Claude spawn reaches it whole, however long, and a stop says whether the agent had started writing
  • A message to a sub-spawn can wait for its turn to end or steer the turn in hand
  • Codex spawns summarise their conversation in the background, as Claude spawns do
  • A saved page can be unpinned from its project's line, and a page on another machine carries a cloud
  • A spawn can open on its pinned concept map, with a switch on each concept
  • Review notes can be saved as a coding task with a title of your own
  • An approved plan's drawing can be moved into a file beside the plan
  • Fixes: a spawn summarising itself while idle read as a finished turn, old plans going up for approval after a failed save, group messages waiting for a whole turn, saved logins not filling pages in the apps, GitLab pages failing in the web view, and the iPhone app cutting off the top of the window
