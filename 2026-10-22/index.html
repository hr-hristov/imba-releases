Update from Settings, Upgrade
Organisations

An organisation of agents

Organisation in New spawn makes a Head spawn and six agents, already in two teams: two leaders under the Head, each leading two agents. Leaders give their agents roles and change them as the work goes, tasks go down and reports come up, and only the Head asks you for more agents. The Org view draws it all as a live chart.

Review

One review for loops and orchestrations

A loop's two plans and an orchestration's run graph are now reviewed the same way: a chart of the proposal, the checks under it, and a note on any part. Each proposal is kept as a version, with what changed since the one before and the planner's answer to each of your notes. Send back carries your notes, and a approves.

Upgrade

The versions imba needs

Settings, Upgrade now says which Claude Code and Codex version each part of imba needs, such as a mod or a model, and marks the ones the installed version is too old for. Install puts the needed version in place, and the row turns up to date.
