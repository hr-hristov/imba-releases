Document card

See what a bot did to a document as the document, not as a diff.

A bot edits a contract and says all five corrections are in. A line diff of a Word file cannot be read at all, and in a markdown one a deadline moved from 30 to 45 days hides among reworded sentences. The document card reads the file itself: it counts the promised corrections, lists the changed numbers first, and draws the page with every change in place.

On a phone, each key on this page is a tap.

01Count

Count the corrections in the file.

Under the bot's reply stands the document card for the file it edited. Its count reads 4 of 5 in red, and under it the one correction promised and not in the file: 8.3, the penalty capped at 10% of the monthly fee. The server reads the file and looks for the words each correction should leave, so the count is never the bot's word for it. On a phone the count stands beside the words added and taken out; a laptop adds what kind each change is and who made it.

02Numbers

See the numbers first.

The Numbers band lists every changed figure before anything else, the old one struck beside the new: 30 to 45 days, 8 to 4 hours, 0.5% to 0.3%, 12 to 24 months. Numbers only, Only on a phone, cuts the page down to the paragraphs holding one; on a laptop v unfolds the card and f does the same. n, or the arrow, steps from number to number, each ringed on the page.

03Page

Read the document as a page.

Unfold, or v, draws the document as a page: inserted words green and underlined, deleted words struck where they stood, changed numbers in their own colour, clause numbers bold. Unchanged paragraphs fold into one line that counts them. Beside each paragraph, or under it on a phone, a note says who made the change, when, and what kind. n and p step through the changes, Change 1 of 8 and on, each ringed. Fold, or v again, folds the card.

04Against

Choose the version to compare against.

The marks are drawn against one version, named on the card: Against: Before this turn in the card's head on a laptop, at its foot on a phone. a, or that button, lists every version: before this turn, the file without its tracked changes, each copy the bot named, such as Sent to client, and each commit of the file. n and p walk the list and Enter picks, or a tap does. Against Sent to client the card holds 10 changes, and two carry Priya Nair's name: the client's lawyer changed the agreement too. The pick is kept on the device for that file.

05Earlier turn

Open an earlier turn's card.

imba keeps the file as each turn left it, and the card reads that copy, so an earlier turn's card keeps showing that turn's work however the file changes later. The Log, l on a laptop or Log in the spawn's menu on a phone, lists the turns, and a turn's number then Enter, or a tap then Full turn, opens one whole. The first turn edited proposal.md, a markdown file, and its card is the same card: u, or Unfold, opens it.

A Word file's headers, footers and footnotes are read too, each under a heading of its own, and Word's tracked changes keep their author and date on every mark.

Every key and button

Each press, and its key.

The chapters above use most of them. Here is all of the document card: what you press, the key that does the same, and what happens. Keys need a keyboard; on a phone or tablet the buttons are taps.

On the spawn

  • UnfoldvUnfolds the newest document card, or folds it.

On an open card

  • FoldvFolds the card back to its count and numbers.
  • Numbers only, or Only on a phonefShows only the paragraphs with a changed number; All changes, or f again, shows every change.
  • The arrowsn pSteps to the next change, or the one before, and rings it.
  • AgainstaOpens the versions the marks can be drawn against, or closes them.
  • qCloses the versions, then folds the card.

In the versions

  • The arrowsn pWalks the list.
  • Pick, or a versionEnterDraws the marks against that version, and keeps the pick for that file.
