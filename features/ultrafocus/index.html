Waiting on you

Only what waits on you

Ctrl+U swaps 29 spawns for the 7 waiting on you, numbered. Automations, Alarms and Tasks go, and one count stands for the 22 at work.

Just enough

Just enough on a row

On a laptop each row says what its last turn set out to do, ticked if it got there and crossed if not. A phone keeps the name. When a spawn at work finishes its turn, its row comes in with a lap round the frame, and the count at work drops by one.

Numbers

A number, then back

1 opens the first spawn. w sends /done and puts it back to work, q comes back, and the list is one shorter. A plan goes the same way with p.

Replies

The proof, not the essay

With Settings, Ultrafocus set to Pictures, films and cards, a reply opens on its proof. t brings back the three paragraphs around it.

Staying

Hard to leave

Ctrl+U again, or the cross on a phone, starts a 15 second count while the list stays as it is. A second press stays in.
