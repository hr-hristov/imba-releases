ReleasesFeatures

Ultrafocus: only what waits on you

One screen with only the spawns waiting on you, numbered. A number opens one, and every other part of imba is a key away.

Turn it on with Ctrl+U, or the crosshair on the bar
Waiting on you

Only the spawns waiting on you

Ctrl+U, or the crosshair on the bar, swaps the whole list for the spawns waiting on you, numbered. The figure at the foot counts the ones at work. Leaving waits 15 seconds, and a second press stays.

Numbers

One number at a time

A number opens that spawn. Done or Approve sends it back to work, q comes back, and the list is one shorter.

Views

Working, Parked and Shells, one list away

w opens the views list: Waiting, Working, Parked, New, Shells and Tasks, each with its count. On a desktop the views you open stand side by side.

New work

Start where the work is

With nothing waiting, New lists the repositories with recent work and the tasks written down under them. f and a row's number open its files, and the number alone opens New spawn there.

Machine

The machine, from the foot of the frame

o opens the Machine list: Clone repository, Monitor, Docker, Agent usage and Files.

Settings

Full screen and larger rows

Settings, Ultrafocus picks full screen or the app bar on this device, the height of a row, what a reply shows, and the sound when a spawn waits.
